# Derde Deva

**Everyday computer troubles and trustworthy, free fixes, written in plain Turkish.**

[Türkçe](README.md)

“Derde deva” is a Turkish idiom for a cure that actually works. This guide is written for people who are not into computers: your mother, your cousin, the shopkeeper down the street. Each page opens with a trouble everyone has had, explains what is going on without jargon, lists what to do step by step, and ends with one fact most people do not know.

If you are the family's tech support, send the page instead of explaining it over the phone again.

## How it is written

- Every recommended program is free.
- The official address of each program is listed at the end of its page. Links are checked with [`araclar/adres-denetle.py`](araclar/adres-denetle.py); the date of the last check is at the bottom of every page.
- Real cases are sourced at the end of the page. Stories marked “Örnek senaryo” are illustrative.
- No ads, no affiliate links, no sponsored recommendations.
- Steps follow Windows 11.
